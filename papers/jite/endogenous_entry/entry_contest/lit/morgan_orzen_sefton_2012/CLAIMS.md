contests", *Economic Theory* 51 (2012), pp. 435–463. Evidence level **[F]**,
published version.

## What is checkable here

- **MOS-C** derived from the payoff function rather than assumed: MOS-1
  recovers `x_n* = (n−1)P/n²` and `π_n* = w + P/n²`; MOS-2 gets the `√(P/F)`
  threshold.
- **MOS-3** reproduces all twelve entries of their Table 1 investment column,
  and **MOS-4** their predicted `n* = 2, 4`. This is the strongest available
  check that §3.1 was read correctly.
- **MOS-A** made arithmetic in MOS-6: `C(6,2) = C(6,4) = 15` distinct
  equilibrium entrant sets, against exactly one under P5.
- **MOS-E** is correct but under-qualified; see `NOTES.md`. MOS-5 records the
  asymmetry the survey omits.
- **MOS-B, MOS-D, MOS-F, MOS-G** confirmed by reading; textual, unchecked.
  None of the statistical analysis was re-run.
