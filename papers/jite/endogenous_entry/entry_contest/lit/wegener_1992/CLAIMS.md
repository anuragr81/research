## What is not claimed

- That the model solves the problem of WG-1 or WG-9. Its closure is by
  property at the gate (Weber's class, p. 927 of *Economy and Society*), not
  by prestige or honor, and its hierarchy is a single contest. It places the
  two in sequence; it does not merge them in Wegener's sense.
- Anything about prestige measurement, consensus or dissensus.
