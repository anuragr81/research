## Use in the manuscript

The author's choice (8 Oct 2026): Tilly supports the outcome, opportunity
hoarding, and Weber (L16) the mechanism, closure. One citation each.

- **The outcome is the same.** The paying class keeps the positions: it
  takes the prize with probability s(k*) (M31), and it is the richest k*
  challengers (M8). In TL-1's terms the resource is the chance to compete
  for the prize, and the bounded set is the paying class.
- **The means differ, and the row says so.** In Tilly the network acts:
  hoarding is "their generally successful strategy" (TL-3), sustained by
  "beliefs and practices" (TL-1), and the resource is created by "the
  hoarding community" (TL-4). Here no one acts and the prize is given; the
  fee rule and the wealth profile produce the same outcome. That is the
  model's point about class: hoarding without a hoarder.
