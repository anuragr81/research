## What the page says

1. **The definition (third paragraph).** If members of a network acquire
   access to a resource that is valuable, renewable, subject to monopoly,
   supportive of network activities, and enhanced by the network's modus
   operandi, they regularly hoard access to it, "creating beliefs and
   practices that sustain their control". The statement is a sufficient
   condition, not a necessary one.
2. **Categorical inequality.** If the network is categorically bounded,
   hoarding contributes to the creation and maintenance of categorical
   inequality.
3. **Hoarding as strategy.** In the preceding examples the term "describes
   their generally successful strategy", and the resources are "largely
   created by the efforts of the hoarding community".
4. **Hoarding versus exploitation.** Hoarding can occur in a "relatively
   pure, independent form", without the exploitation it often complements.
5. **Enforcement.** Members reinforce control over hoarded resources through
   their power to include or exclude others (last paragraph).
