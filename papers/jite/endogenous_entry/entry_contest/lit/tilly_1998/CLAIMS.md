# Claims about Tilly (1998)

**Source.** *Durable Inequality*, p. 154, read from the author's photograph
of the page [P] (see `RECONSTRUCTION.md`). Cited in `MANUSCRIPT.tex` (row
L19) and in `refs.bib` as `Tilly1998`. The quotations below were transcribed
by me from the image, with line-end hyphens closed ("opportu- nity",
"activi- ties"). `Tilly1998.lean` records the logical form of the definition,
a sufficient condition, with a control showing it is not a necessary one.

## What is not claimed

- That the paying class is a network or a category in Tilly's sense. It is
  the set of challengers whose wealth makes the fee affordable, and the rule
  draws its boundary.
- That the five conditions of TL-1 are necessary for hoarding; Tilly states
  them as sufficient (`control_not_necessary`).
- Anything from the rest of the book.
