# Claims about Weber (1978)

**Source.** Max Weber, *Economy and Society*, ed. Roth and Wittich,
University of California Press, 1978. Sections read in full: pp. 43-44,
302-307, 926-937 (see `RECONSTRUCTION.md`). Evidence level **[F] for those
sections**. Quotations are as printed; OCR noise in the scan was checked
against the context and is not reproduced. `verify_weber.py` matches each
quotation against the scan with a tolerance for OCR errors.

| WB-1 | 927 | "The mode of distribution, in accord with the law of marginal utility, excludes the non-wealthy from competing for highly valued goods" | Wealth acts on competition through the marginal utility of property. In the model this is the utility cost of the fee, `κ(w) = u(w) − u(w−c)`, which falls with wealth and makes entry richest-first | M5, M23 |
| WB-2 | 306 | "Money and an entrepreneurial position are not in themselves status qualifications, although they may lead to them" | Status is distinct from class. In the model wealth sets only the cost of entry, and the prize is won by rank in a contest whose draws do not depend on wealth | M26 |
| WB-3 | 43 | "participation of certain persons is excluded, limited, or subjected to conditions" | Closure. The fee subjects participation to a condition, and M4 gives entrants the interest in closure that Weber describes, since each further entrant lowers their gain | M4, M10 |
