## Verdict for C12

Not a precedent for M28; a contrast that sharpens its scope.

- **Opposite sign.** In their contest, participation falls as ranking becomes
  more accurate (Proposition 1, MTV-2 to MTV-4). Translated, their accuracy is
  the weight on the costly choice, `t`; M28 says the count does not fall as
  `t` rises. Opposite directions.
- **Why.** Their agents choose effort at a convex cost. Accuracy intensifies
  competition until rents are gone, and then some drop out. The model here
  has no effort choice: the fee is fixed, and non-payers still compete on the
  base score. So the falsifier for M28 is an effort margin, and the
  manuscript should say so: M28's sign is a property of fixed-cost entry with
  non-payers competing.
- **Consistent with the expected Drugov-Ryvkin analogue.** Drugov and Ryvkin
  (2020), by its abstract, find effort falls as noise becomes more dispersed;
  M28 has the same sign for the entry margin. So the extensive margin here
  moves like their intensive margin, and unlike MTV's extensive margin, which
  is driven by effort.
- **Their report of Fu, Jiao and Lu (MTV-6).** With effort among entrants
  (Tullock), more noise can encourage entry, the same direction as MTV. Our
  own record of Fu, Jiao and Lu does not cover this; it is cited only as
  MTV's report.

## A parallel worth noting

With heterogeneous abilities, participation is single-crossing in ability
(Proposition 4, MTV-5): a threshold type, above which all participate. The
model's threshold is in wealth (M5). The structure is the same; the sorting
variable differs, and here it is not merit.
