Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript entitled “Signaling in Education and Inequality: A Model of Social Capital Investments” for consideration in The B.E. Journal of Economic Analysis & Policy. The paper develops a theoretical framework analysing how income inequality shapes social capital investments, with a particular focus on the signaling value of education. By endogenising status within small-scale competitions, the model links relative income, participation costs, and status-driven behavior to long-term mobility outcomes. The analysis yields three key results: (1) moderate inequality incentivises social capital investments, but excessive inequality renders competitions unsustainable; (2) rising inequality endogenously elevates participation costs, limiting mobility; and (3) the model features only two stable equilibria—universal participation or rich-only exclusion.


These findings illuminate the tension between equality of opportunity and outcome, offering policy insights on managing education's signaling role to mitigate inequality traps. I believe it will be of interest to BEJEAP’s readership, as it combines formal modeling with clear implications for inequality and education policy.

I can confirm that this manuscript is original and has not been published elsewhere- nor is it under consideration by any other journal. 

Thank you for considering this work. I look forward to your feedback.
