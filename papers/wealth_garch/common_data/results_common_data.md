# The common bank dataset: fetch and coverage

Fetched 13 October 2026 under PREREG_common_data.md (commit fa17bd36), by
fetch_common_data.py (commit 0831cc28), after the pre-registration was
committed. 41 files, one per year 1984-2024, 1,656,057 rows in all, the
largest 5,084,940 bytes, all committed. Re-fetching 2024 on the same day gave a
byte-identical file (same sha256).

## Findings

- All five pre-registered coverage expectations hold (E1 to E3 below).
  Nothing after 31 December 2024 was returned.
- `RWAJT` is present for every commercial bank in every quarter from 1990,
  including 2020-2024. The expected fall from 2020 appears as zeros, not
  as empty values: in December 2020, 2022 and 2024, 1,682, 1,449 and 1,487
  commercial banks (38.1%, 34.8% and 37.4%) report `RWAJT` = 0 with
  `RBCRWAJ` empty or zero, which are the banks that elect the community
  bank leverage ratio. Every test must treat `RWAJT` <= 0 as missing, as
  stage 4 of the Peura and Keppo replication did.
- `RBCT2` is empty before 1990 and `EQCTRSTX` (net treasury stock
  transactions) is empty before 2001, for every bank.
- `EDEPFOR` is present for under 2% of banks, those with foreign offices;
  an empty value there means no such deposits.
- The common sample has 14,801 banks in 1984 and 4,056 in 2024.
