## The model

- Section 2: net cash flow g and deposits D are correlated geometric Brownian
  motions; equity per unit of deposits V(k), k = g/D, by homogeneity
  (Proposition 1). Equity holders close at k* (11), the regulator would close
  at k** (10), trading lump-sum bankruptcy costs against monitoring;
  closure is max{k**, k*} without subsidies (Proposition 4).
- Section 3: subsidies that implement k** at least cost (Propositions 6, 7),
  subsidies that remove the convexity of equity (Propositions 8 to 10), and
  equity support near closure (Proposition 11).
