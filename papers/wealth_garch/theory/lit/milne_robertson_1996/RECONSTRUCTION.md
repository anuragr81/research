## The model

- Section 2: cash $dx=(rx+\mu-d)dt+\sigma dz$, dividends $d\ge0$ (no issuance),
  liquidation at $x=0$ with value zero, objective the discounted dividends at
  $\rho>r$. Dividends at a barrier $x^*$ (Proposition 1); $V$ concave with
  $V'\ge1$ below it (Proposition 2).
- Section 3: output chosen, local risk aversion $W=-V''/V'$ positive and
  falling in $x$ (Proposition 3, Theorem 2).
- Section 4: the contrast with discrete-time risk-loving near bankruptcy.
- Sections 5 and 6: investment by an amended q, and aggregation.
