\section{Scope}

The map names every object of the model of the enhancement paper once, and the
manuscript uses these names and no others. Seven names are those of the parent
bundle's terminology table, bank, supervisor, insurer, investors, discount
rate, dividend barrier and reference. The other 22 were agreed with the author
for this paper on 28 October 2026. Inputs are what the model takes as given, internal objects are
built from the inputs inside the model, and outputs are what the rows predict.
Directly observed is Yes when a record of the world gives the object without
any computation, which is when the last column begins with Recorded, and No
otherwise. The other status words are Elicited, Computed, Assumed, Never
observed and A device. Every computed object names its recovery formula and
the Lean theorem that carries it, in \path{lean/mathlib/}. The data are the
FDIC Call Report files of \path{common_data/}, and the facts are those fixed in
\path{comparison/PREREG_comparison.md}. In the assumptions, the letter $d$ is
the differential, $t$ is time and $i$ indexes banks, and none of them is an
object of the model.
