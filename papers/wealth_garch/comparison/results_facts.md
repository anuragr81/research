## Findings

Buffer over R = 10.5%, measured in units of each bank's own earnings
volatility sigma_i (2005-2014), on 2015Q1-2024Q4.

| fact | sample A (668 banks) | sample B (61 largest) |
|---|---|---|
| F1, dividends paid from a buffer below 1 sigma_i | 1.6% (222 of 14,112) | 4.8% (78 of 1,612) |
| F2, buffer above 4 sigma_i | 83.2% (16,573 of 19,920) | 61.6% (1,342 of 2,177) |
| F3, recapitalisations starting below 1 sigma_i | 11.0% (34 of 310) | 15.2% (7 of 46) |
| F4, recapitalisations landing between 1 and 4 sigma_i | 22.9% (71 of 310) | 30.4% (14 of 46) |

Banks almost never pay dividends from a thin buffer, mostly hold capital
far above R, recapitalise mostly from buffers of 1 sigma_i or more, and
most issues land outside the band of 1 to 4 sigma_i. Every fact has 20 or more observations in both samples.
