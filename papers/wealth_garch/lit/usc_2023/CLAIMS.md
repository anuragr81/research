# Claims: United States Code (2023), 12 U.S.C. 1831o

Bib key `usc1831o`. Source: section 38 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act, 12 U.S.C. 1831o (prompt corrective action) and 1831o-1 (source of strength), United States Code, 2023 edition, GovInfo. Printed pages 1161 to 1171, read in full. Evidence [F].

## Quotations

| ID | Page | Quotation | What we rely on |
|---|---|---|---|
| PCA-Q1 | 1161 | The purpose of this section is to resolve the problems of insured depository institutions at the least possible long-term loss to the Deposit Insurance Fund. | Why the supervisor and the insurer enforce capital: to limit the insurer's loss (N6, N7). |
| PCA-Q2 | 1161 | shall carry out the purpose of this section by taking prompt corrective action to resolve the problems of insured depository institutions. | Both the supervisor and the insurer, in its capacity as insurer, are charged with the actions (N6, N7). |
| PCA-Q3 | 1162 | a distribution of cash or other property by any insured depository institution or company to its owners made on account of that ownership | A capital distribution is the model's dividend paid to investors (A1). |
| PCA-Q4 | 1162 | not less than 2 percent of total assets | The critical capital level, tangible equity to total assets, is at least 2% (A10). |
| PCA-Q5 | 1163 | An insured depository institution shall make no capital distribution if, after making the distribution, the institution would be undercapitalized. | The supervisor's restriction on dividends (A9). |
| PCA-Q6 | 1164 | Requiring the institution to sell enough shares or obligations of the institution so that the institution will be adequately capitalized after the sale. | The supervisor can order a recapitalisation (A9). |
| PCA-Q7 | 1164 | Restricting the interest rates that the institution pays on deposits to the prevailing rates of interest on deposits of comparable amounts and maturities | The supervisor can restrict what the bank pays its creditors (A9). |
| PCA-Q8 | 1166 | appoint a receiver (or, with the concurrence of the Corporation, a conservator) for the institution | Closure of a critically undercapitalized bank (A10). |
