## Consequences for this bundle

- Contrary on regulation (GH09-Q1, GH09-Q15, GH09-Q16), but tested against
  the Basel Tier 1 minimum on large listed banks, 1991-2004; Orozco and
  Rubio (OR-Q3) read this as the wrong line. Their own cutoff of 6% Tier 1
  is the well-capitalised Tier 1 line, and there they find regulation
  matters (GH09-Q8).
- Size: same sign as ours (GH09-Q9); their access reading (GH09-Q10) is
  what results_access.md tests and does not support, on banks their sample
  excludes (GH09-Q4).
- Bank fixed effects (GH09-Q12, GH09-Q13) are the antecedent for persistent
  bank-specific levels; a margin common to all banks, as in
  results_do_nothing.md, is not a fixed effect.
- Nothing on banks' own actions damping the movement of capital ratios.
