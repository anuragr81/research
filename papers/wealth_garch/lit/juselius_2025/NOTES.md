## Consequences for the enhancement paper

- The closest measurement to C6: a buffer of about 3.8 shock standard
  deviations after the crisis (4.7 for US banks). Theirs is target over the
  volatility of buffer changes net of the bank's own actions, for 34 large
  groups, from a partial adjustment; ours is the volatility a barrier model
  needs to reproduce buffers, against earnings, stress-test and market
  measures, for all US commercial banks. Cite and contrast both numerator and
  denominator.
- C5 is not in it: one reduced-form model, no published models scored, no
  out-of-sample test.
- Supports the floor as minimum plus buffers (JPT-Q2).
