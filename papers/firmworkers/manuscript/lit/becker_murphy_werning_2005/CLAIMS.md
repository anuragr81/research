## Readings recorded

- BMW-D1 (p.23): the printed `F∗(y) = (α/(ω̄^c κ))^δ y^{1/δ}` has exponent `δ`
  on the constant; inverting the printed `y∗(s)` gives `(α/(ω̄^c κ))^{1/δ}`
  (`Fstar_inverts_ystar`). With `α = ½`, `β = 1`, `ω̄ = 1` the printed form at
  the top of the support is `√6/36`, not 1 (`control_printed_Fstar_misses_top`).
  The density inherits the same slip.
- BMW-D2 (p.23): the printed support `[0, ω̄^c κ]` is the range of `c∗`; the
  range of `y∗` is `[0, κω̄^c/α]` (`ystar_top`).
- BMW-D3 (p.23): "for β large δ tends to zero" — `δ = β/(1 − α)` rises with
  `β`; it is `1/δ` that tends to zero, which is what makes the density behave as
  `1/y` (`control_density_tail_needs_large_delta` shows `δ = 1` gives a flat
  density). The conclusion stands; the wording is a slip. The JPE version may
  have corrected these; it was not read.
