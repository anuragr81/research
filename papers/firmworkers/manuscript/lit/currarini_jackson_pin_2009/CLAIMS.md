## Readings recorded

  read as `t₁ = √3/(8c²)`, `t₂ = (3 − √3)/(8c²)`, which gives the printed
  `q₁ = √3 − 1` (`example_solution`).
- CJP-D2: the closure link is ours, not CJP's: they do not use a single
  cross-group weight, and their biased process `q₁^β + q₂^β = 1` (7) is a
  different functional form.
