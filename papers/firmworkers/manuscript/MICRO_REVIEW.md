# Review of the source's micro claims against the micro dynamics (pass 1, 12 Oct 2026)

The source states its micro conclusions as a summary ("It follows from the dynamics…", appendix, `inputs/firmworkers_corrected.tex` lines 382–436). With the macro model deferred, each claim is judged here only by what follows from the micro model as decided. Nothing in `claims.yaml` has been changed; the verdicts are proposals for discussion.

## The micro dynamics used

One period, the one-shot comparison Q5 left to tractability. A displaced worker with former income `x_i` and loss `ℓ = x_i − x_0` chooses one of three routes:

| Route | Income | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Downgrade | `x_0` (bottom rung) | M32, reading (a) |
| Return to the former job | `x_i − κ ℓ^b` | M7's cost `(x_i − x_0)^b`, with a scale `κ` (`κ = 1` is issue 1's option (a)) |
| Lottery | `x_k − L`, `k` uniform over the other entrants | M11, M14, M32 |

Results are form-free (any nondecreasing `u`) where marked; otherwise risk-neutral. All Lean is in `lean/IscLean/Routes.lean` (28 theorems, axiom-clean, built and audited by `check.py --lean`), plus M33–M35 in `Displacement.lean`. `Routes.lean` is not yet listed in the manuscript because no ledger row cites it.

## What this changes for the open issues

- **M32 matters most.** The source's sorting claim (M20) holds under a proportional step and fails under the bottom rung, for every increasing `u`. JLS also point to a proportional step. M32 stays decided unless reopened, but this is the strongest reason so far to look at it again.
- **A new open issue: the reading of route (ii).** Return to one's own former job (line 384, the ledger's reading) against claiming any higher position from a worker willing to step down (line 386, a two-sided swap market). Under the first, M21's cost part and claim 9 are rejected; under the second they could hold, but the market needs a model (matching of up-movers and down-movers), and nothing in Lean covers it.
- **Issue 1 (unit)** now decides claim 1 outright and the turning point in claims 6 and 7.
- **Issue 2 (stakes)** gains a result: option (a)'s claim is false in general and true at the top and below the pool mean. A further option is to restrict `b` (counterexamples need `b` near 1).
- **Shape of `u` (issue 5)** cannot rescue M20 under the bottom rung, since M33 is form-free.

## Pass 2 (14 Oct 2026): the steady state

The author's decision (M39): one type of player, the worker, in two states; uniform displacement at rate `d`; the steady state as the object; routes compared by lifetime value `V(x) = (x + βdW)/(1 − β(1 − d))`, which is affine in income with slope the lifetime multiplier `A = 1/(1 − β(1 − d)) ≥ 1` (`SteadyState.lean`, audited). Two consequences for every one-shot result: comparisons that hold for any nondecreasing utility of income carry over unchanged, because `V` rises with income; comparisons of a one-time cost with an income gain change, because the gain is multiplied by `A` while the cost is not (issue 13).
