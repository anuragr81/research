**(c) Most important BIR problems**

1. **Version and pagination mismatch (B1).** Every pinpoint page comes from the Jan-2019 working paper, but the bibliography cites AER 109(10) 3395–3436. In a manuscript these page numbers would be wrong. Either cite the working paper or re-map the pages to the AER text, which I could not check.
2. **The "defect of the evaluator / person-based vs process-based" framing is contradicted by BIR's own model (B33, B32).** BIR's impartial type has correct beliefs, no animus and Bayesian coherence, yet discriminates, because of the endogenous informational content of the history (p.20). BIR also do not present their taxonomy as exhaustive. Both claims are live in TDP §3 and §7.
3. **Prop 2 is misdescribed as being about exogenous likelihoods (B20, B21).** check_pinning's docstring says so outright, and TDP implies it. BIR's fn 10 says exactly the reverse: the exogenous case is trivial, and Prop 2 exists because histories are endogenously informative.
4. **"Unstated scope condition" (B18).** Bayes' rule is an explicit assumption on p.11, so "unstated" is false. Entry 9's headline claim should not be reused.
5. **Entry 5's mechanism for the reversal ("biased priors overshooting") is wrong (B36)** and is still uncorrected in the log.
6. **Wrong page for the aggregate Prop-1 formula (B8: p.17, not p.16) and for the "posterior mean" quote (B15: p.19, not p.18).**
7. **Overstatements that leave out BIR's other results (B10, B14, B28, B38).** "Only as judgment becomes perfectly objective" ignores Prop 2's attenuation along histories. "Requires two types" ignores BIR's "one possible way", and "identifies bias" ignores "some form of misspecification". B10's wording is in the proposed manuscript text (plan 3.D).

**(c) Most important Heckman problems**

1. **"No sample size repairs" is not Heckman's (H5a).** It appears as a description of what he showed in TDP:243-244 and in the proposed manuscript insertion (plan 3.C, lines 894-899). Heckman is silent on sample size, and his only size-related remark (small applicant pools, p.108) points the other way. Rephrase it as the author's inference, e.g. "a bias that, being in the estimand, does not diminish with more audits".
2. **"Nothing guarantees" is on p.109, not p.102 (H2).** PE's only locator, and its bibliography note "quotation from p.102", cover two quotes on two different pages.
3. **The p.102 sentence is not specifically about threshold hiring (H3).** Cite pp.109-111 and 113-115 for the threshold mechanism.
