**(c) Most important problems (Hawthorne)**
1. **H2.** `Hawthorne2004` is cited twice in the manuscript but is missing from `bibliography.bib`, so the compiled PDF has an undefined citation (log lines 1196, 1200). The entry was drafted in plan B.A but never applied.
2. **H33.** The recurring claim that Hawthorne's illustration is "one basis cued twice" and so misses an attribute-local model is contradicted by his own two-basis medical example. The quoted concerns follow directly from that example, and it shows the overwrite Q_e[E]=Q_fe[E] on distinct bases.
3. **H17.** H-README says the LR example uses "exactly the same … reports" read as Bayes factors. Hawthorne changes the reports: LR .50 and 2, not the Bayes-factor reading of .90, which would be 1/9 and 9.
4. **H30/H29.** The manuscript footnote's "under his order-free variants the sequence effect disappears" needs "across distinct bases". Only the Basis-Commuting Version is fully order-free, and "repairs" overstates what he does.
5. **H26/H24.** Hawthorne (note 20) gives the name "Bayes factor" to LR factors. The manuscript's usage (Bayes factor = NL factor), with Hawthorne cited, inverts his terminology. His taxonomy also has three members, and NL is not an "end".
6. **H34.** Plan text attributes the partial-adoption-weight alternative to Hawthorne's objection. That is not in the paper.
7. **H18/H19/H20.** The Reordering Theorem is paraphrased with r "free", when the theorem pins it. The sympy check prints a ratio that is not Hawthorne's r, and the dialectic calls the theorem "verified" when one instance was checked.
8. **H7.** Minor: the "it seems implausible…" quotation starts on p. 98, not p. 99 (dialectic:91, 213). **H10:** Lange silently dropped from note 15. **H32:** Hawthorne classes Döring as a defender of Standard Sequential Updating (notes 2, 10).

**(c) Most important problems (Weisberg)**
1. **W17.** Citing Weisberg p. 9 ("sets aside") to house the ω=1 delivered-credence model misreads him. He calls non-commutativity on input distributions *desirable* only because inputs *should* change with order (Lange).
