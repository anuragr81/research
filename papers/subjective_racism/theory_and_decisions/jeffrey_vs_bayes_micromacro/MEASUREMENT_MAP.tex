\noindent The model has one evaluator, who holds a prior over two binary
attributes of a candidate and reads two documents, each bearing on one
attribute. Each document leaves an impression, a credence about its own
attribute, and the evaluator revises by Jeffrey conditioning. A population of
evaluators with the same prior and impressions reads the documents in one of two
sequences. An observer, the auditor, reads statistics of the population's beliefs
and decisions and compares them with the sequence-free benchmark. The objects
fall into three tables. Inputs are fixed before any document is read, internal
objects are built from the inputs inside the model, including devices of the
proofs, and outputs are what the results predict. The desirability vector, the
outside option and every object built from them are the modeller's constructs,
attributed to the evaluator to rationalise recorded engagement decisions, rather
than quantities in the world. The beliefs are the evaluator's own and are
obtained, when they are obtained at all, by asking.

\section*{Recovery formulas}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Prior covariance.} From an elicited prior table,
  $c=P_{00}P_{11}-P_{01}P_{10}$ (\lean{assoc\_prior}).
\item \emph{Impressions.} The belief about the attribute of the cue read
  first, elicited right after that cue, is its delivered credence
  (\lean{jeffreyA\_pins\_mA1}, \lean{jeffreyB\_pins\_mB1}).
\item \emph{Adoption weight.} With $m$ the marginal of the attribute read
  second before its cue, $m'$ the same marginal after it and $x$ its delivered
  credence, $\omega=1-(m'-x)/(m-x)$ whenever $m\neq x$
  (\lean{omega\_recovery}). When $m=x$ the cue moves nothing and $\omega$ is not
  identified.
\item \emph{Benchmark.} $\PB$ is the Jeffrey update of $P$ whose targets are
  the marginals the Bayes factors imply, computed from $P$, $q$ and $r$
  (\lean{PB} and its closed forms).
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Structure}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Elicited.} The prior marginals, the prior covariance, the
  impressions and the posterior tables. Each needs a question to the
  evaluator. The covariance and the posterior tables need a question about both
  traits at once.
\item \emph{Recorded.} The reading sequence and the engagement decisions.
\item \emph{Computed.} The prior table, the Bayes factors, the benchmark, the
  sequence effect, the gaps, the mean belief, the distortions, the adoption
  weight and the protection class.
\item \emph{Constructs, not observed.} The desirability vector, the outside
  option, the score, the surplus and its density, and the surplus-weighted
  loss. Each rationalises recorded engagement decisions.
\item \emph{Never observed.} The attributes themselves, the decision flip and
  the share of decisions changed.
\item \emph{Devices.} The route directions and their coefficients, the
  normaliser, the association coefficient of partial adoption and the score
  coefficient.
\end{itemize}
