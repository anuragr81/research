# Arrow (1971), notes

## What was verified

- Ten quotations from the 1971 working paper are found on the PDF pages
  they cite in the OCR text, compared with whitespace removed (LC-2). One
  carries a recorded elision (AR-Q8).
- Eqs. (13) and (14) and our derivation of the difference mode are Lean
  theorems in the paper's notation (LC-4).
- Our reading is consistent with the working paper on these points. The
  published chapter of 1973 is not read, and the record cites the working
  paper.

## The parallel with Paper A, and where it ends

1. **Same.** Beliefs about a group can be self-confirming when the group's
   qualification responds to the return the beliefs produce (AR-Q7, AR-Q8).
   Paper A's trap is a low standing that the discrimination it induces
   reproduces (M5, M6).
2. **Same.** Where there are costs of change the outcome depends on history
   (AR-Q3), as Paper A's basins do (M6).
3. **Different.** Arrow's discriminatory equilibria are suggested by the
   instability of the symmetric one and not shown to exist. Paper A's are
   exhibited in closed form (M5).
4. **No erosion benchmark.** The paper leaves open how employers' knowledge
   modifies their estimates (AR-Q10), and nothing in it has counter-examples
   eroding a stereotype. Together with Phelps (1972), this removes the
   support for the predecessor's reading of the zero-exclusion case as such
   a benchmark.
