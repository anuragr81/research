# Jeffrey (1983), notes

## What was verified

- Eight quotations are found on the PDF pages they cite in the OCR text,
  compared with whitespace removed (LC-2).
- (11-5), (11-7), (11-8) in both directions, the mudrunner numbers, and the
  failure of commutativity are Lean theorems (LC-4).
- Our reading is consistent with the second edition on these points.

## The bearing on Paper A

1. **The attribution in Appendix D.** The rule the predecessor calls Jeffrey
   conditioning is (11-2) and (11-7), under the rigidity (11-1) and (11-8).
   The second edition attributes the chapter to the 1957 dissertation and
   says it is unchanged from the first edition except for minor corrections.
   The first edition itself is not read.
2. **Why the rule adds nothing within one reading.** (11-7) mixes the
   conditionals on the atoms by the new atom weights. Paper A's M2 and M3
   show the Bayesian posterior on class under group sufficiency is such a
   mixture, and every such mixture is a Bayesian posterior. Jeffrey himself
   calls conditionalization a limiting case of the rule (JF-Q5).
3. **What lies outside the model.** Two applications need not commute
   (JF-Q6, JF-Q7, `control_not_commutative`). Paper A has one reading of a
   marker, so the order effect is outside it rather than refuted by it.
